Step 2 · Edges (4 cases)
Ua-Perm
M2 U M U2 M' U M2
M2 U M U2 M' U M2
Pure M-slice + U rhythm. 3 edges cycle counter-clockwise (viewed from top).
Ub-Perm
M2 U' M U2 M' U' M2
M2 U' M U2 M' U' M2
Exact inverse of Ua: swap every U for U'. 3 edges cycle clockwise.
H-Perm
M2 U M2 U2 M2 U M2
M2 U M2 U2 M2 U M2
All M2s. Symmetric — opposite edges swap in pairs.
Z-Perm
M2 U M2 U M' U2 M2 U2 M'
M2 U M2 U M' U2 M2 U2 M'
Adjacent edges swap in pairs. Recognize by "checkerboard" side colors.
